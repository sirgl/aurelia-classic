# Encyclopedia texts: provenance and formula audit

State of 21 October 2026 (branch `publish/pedia-text-audit`, cut from `origin/main` 5d719994). The player-visible texts of the classic
Encyclopedia screen (`classic/crates/classic-ui/src/ui/pedia/`, `screen_pedia.rs`) were checked for two questions: where does each
text family come from, and does any page state how something is *calculated*.

Classes: **a** generated by code from rule data (numbers, names, typed effects); **b** our own wording; **c** looks like the help
text of the original game or is closely paraphrased from it (not allowed); **d** text of Unciv (allowed with attribution, MPL-2.0).

Method (all numbers [measured] in this branch): the English bundles `classic/assets/lang/en/classic-pedia-*.ftl` were read in full;
every message was compared with all JSON files, the English translation template, the documents of a shallow read-only clone of the
Unciv repository (commit of 21 October 2026, 92 text files, 158,519 distinct five-word runs; script kept outside the repository) by
five-word and three-word runs; the git history of the files was read; a dump of all 1,064 generated pages was read for numbers,
fractions and expressions. The original game's own texts are not available to this project, so class **c** is a judgement of
wording and structure, not a diff; where that judgement is uncertain the sentence was rewritten.

## 2. Formulas and constants removed (the pages keep ready values and short qualitative explanations)

| Family | What was removed | Count |
|---|---|---:|
| Concept articles | nothing (no formula was present) | 0 |
| Era pages | row "Faith cost multiplier" (`stat-faith-cost-multiplier`) | 1 |
| Difficulty pages | rows "AI growth", "AI unit training", "AI construction", "AI unit supply bonus" (a table of constants the AI computes with); the player rows (cost percentages, happiness, free supply) stay as ready values | 4 |
| Technology pages | "Road steps cost N/60 of a move" became "Movement along roads costs less/more" (`tech-road-steps` split into `tech-roads-faster`, `tech-roads-slower`) | 1 (+1 key) |
| Unit abilities | base value and per-citizen multiplier of hurrying production; base gold and gold per era of trade missions; "N times your current tourism" of a concert; "previous N turns of output" of discovering a technology and of a political treatise: now one qualitative sentence each | 7 |
| Unit stats | "N% of damage dealt" city plunder; capital-defence fall-off "points per tile"; "N% of the victim's strength" golden-age points; "N% of strength lost per turn" of religious units | 4 |
| Modifiers | "yield equal to N% of the strength of each enemy killed"; "N% of excess happiness becomes culture" | 2 |

Total: 19 messages changed or removed in each of the 7 languages. Kept on purpose, as ready values of the thing shown: costs,
strengths, movement, yields, "+N% production toward world wonders", durations ("a golden age of 8 turns"), chances.

## 3. Sentences written again in our own words (class c judgement)

Eight sentences whose structure followed the help text style of the original game: "Can drive other religions out of a city",
"Can be bought only once your religion is enhanced", "While it is trained, the city's spare Food adds to Production",
"With N of these you may choose an ideology", "Comes with a free Great Work of Art", "Shields your nation from the tourism bonus
other nations get from the Internet", "Spies already gathering intelligence here feel its espionage effects at once",
"Other nations get N% of the tourism a concert gives". The other six languages were translated again from the new English
(machine drafts like the rest of the bundles; native review is still open, see `platform/docs/localization.md`).

## 4. Word rename

The old screen name is no longer used in code, identifiers (`Screen::Encyclopedia`), the shortcut id (`encyclopedia`, key
`shortcut.encyclopedia`), bundle headers and comments of `classic-ui`: 56 lines in 18 files [measured]. Documents under
`classic/docs/` (`README.md`, `SOURCES.md`, `RESOURCING.md`, `UNCIV-ATTRIBUTION.md`, `rename-classic.md`, `NOTICE`) still name it as
a source they refuse; the branch `refactor/classic-unciv-formulas` rewrites those and was not touched here.
